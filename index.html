The drive

Not a track. Not a parking lot. A road.

Frederick County has the best driving roads within an hour of Washington. Ridge climbs with proper switchbacks, valley straights between stone walls, a covered bridge or two. We meet in Frederick, run a briefing, and hand you the keys.

The instructor rides with you the whole way. They know every corner and every pothole, and they will tell you when to breathe.

  • Rear-wheel drive, naturally aspirated, 8,500 rpm
  • Speed limits apply. The roads are the point
  • Photos of you with the car, included

How it works

Four steps.
One of them is the good part.

  1. 1

    Pick a drive and a date

    Choose Driver I, II or III. Tell us a date and which car. We confirm within a day.

  2. 2

    Meet us in Frederick

    Twenty minutes: waiver, licence check, the briefing, seat and mirrors. Then the start button.

  3. 3

    Drive

    Your hands, our instructor beside you. The valley first, the ridge if you booked it. Nobody hurries you.

  4. 4

    Photos, keys, debrief

    We shoot you with the car at the turnaround and send the photos that evening.

Questions

Asked before.

Do I actually drive, or is it a ride-along?

You drive. An instructor sits in the passenger seat the whole time. If you would rather ride, book Driver X and say so.

Is it on a track?

No. Every drive is on public backroads in Frederick County. Speed limits apply. The roads do the work, and they are very good roads.

Can I bring someone?

The car has two seats and one of them is the instructor's. Guests are welcome at the start point, and on Driver III they ride in the follow car for the whole loop.

What if it rains?

We reschedule at no charge. A wet road and a rear-wheel-drive V10 are not a good afternoon, and we would rather you came back on a dry one.

Which car do I get?

You choose when you book: the purple Huracán or the green one. They are mechanically identical. If you want both, that is Driver X.

Do I need my own insurance?

No. Cover is included in the price. Your liability is capped at a deductible we confirm in writing before you pay.

Can I drive a manual?

The Tecnica is a seven-speed dual-clutch with paddles. There is no clutch pedal, and you will not miss it above 6,000 rpm.

Is it a gift?

It can be. Any tier is available as a voucher, valid for a year. We send it as a printable card the same day.
