05 — Before you drive Draft · numbers pending client sign-off

The short version

The full terms come with your booking. These are the ones that decide whether you can drive.

Minimum age
25 to drive. 18 to ride along. Under 18 rides with a parent or guardian in the chase car.
Licence
A full, valid driver's licence held for at least 3 years. Bring it on the day. No licence, no drive.
Deposit
A refundable security deposit is held on a card before you drive and released after. The amount is set per car and confirmed at booking.
Waiver
Every driver and passenger signs a waiver on the day. You get a copy to read with your confirmation.
Weather
Rain, ice or snow and we move your date. No charge to rebook.
