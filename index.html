Questions

Asked before.

Do I actually drive, or is it a ride-along?

You drive. An instructor sits in the passenger seat the whole time. If you would rather ride, book Driver X and say so.

Is it on a track?

No. Every drive is on public backroads in Frederick County. Speed limits apply. The roads do the work, and they are very good roads.

Can I bring someone?

The car has two seats and one of them is the instructor's. Guests are welcome at the start point, and on Driver III they ride in the follow car for the whole loop.

What if it rains?

We reschedule at no charge. A wet road and a rear-wheel-drive V10 are not a good afternoon, and we would rather you came back on a dry one.

Which car do I get?

You choose when you book: the purple Huracán or the green one. They are mechanically identical. If you want both, that is Driver X.

Do I need my own insurance?

No. Cover is included in the price. Your liability is capped at a deductible we confirm in writing before you pay.

Can I drive a manual?

The Tecnica is a seven-speed dual-clutch with paddles. There is no clutch pedal, and you will not miss it above 6,000 rpm.

Is it a gift?

It can be. Any drive is available as a voucher, valid for a year. We send it as a printable card the same day.
