FAQ
How does my recipient redeem the voucher?

They receive a card, physical or digital, with a QR code. They call or email to schedule their drive. Vouchers are good for 12 months.

What's the difference between Pilot and Copilot?

Pilot means they drive, with a professional in the passenger seat. Copilot means a professional drives while they ride along.

Can the experience be customized?

Yes. Driver X covers extra time, weddings, content shoots, proms, and other custom requests.

Where does the drive happen?

We meet in Frederick, Maryland and drive local back roads across the DC, Maryland, and Northern Virginia area.
